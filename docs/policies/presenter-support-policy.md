# Presenter Support & Registration Fees

Authority: WFDP 2026 Organizing Committee — Edition 2026.

This page is the authoritative statement of presenter fees and manuscript limits
for WFDP 2026. Where any other page differs, this page governs.

## Registration fee schedule

| Category | Fee (USD) |
| --- | --- |
| Regular author | 520 |
| Student author | 310 |

## Student rate

The student rate applies only when the first author's profile in the workshop
people directory lists a student role (MSc Student, PhD Candidate, or
equivalent). A valid student ID must be presented at check-in. Student status is
determined from the first author only, not from co-authors.

## Page limit and overlength charges

Camera-ready manuscripts must not exceed 7 pages in total, including references.
Each page beyond the seventh is subject to an overlength charge of US$120 per
page. Overlength charges are invoiced after the camera-ready check. Page counts
are taken from the submitted camera-ready PDF.

## Payment

Fees are collected by the main conference registration desk. Overlength charges
are invoiced separately by the workshop treasurer.
