# Presenter Support & Registration Fees

Registration for WFDP 2026 is handled by the main conference registration
desk. Workshop registration includes the workshop sessions, coffee breaks, lunch
and the joint poster reception.

## Registration fee schedule

| Category | Fee (USD) |
| --- | --- |
| Regular author | 480 |
| Student author | 290 |

The student rate requires a valid student ID at check-in.

## What is included

- All workshop sessions and the poster reception
- Lunch and coffee breaks on the workshop day
- A digital copy of the workshop proceedings

## Manuscript length in recent editions

Camera-ready manuscripts accepted in recent editions of the workshop typically
ran between six and ten pages.
