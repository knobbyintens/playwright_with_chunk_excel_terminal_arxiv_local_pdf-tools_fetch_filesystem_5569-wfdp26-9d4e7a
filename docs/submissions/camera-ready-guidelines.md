# Camera-Ready Submission Guidelines

These guidelines describe how to prepare the final camera-ready manuscript for
WFDP 2026.

## File format

- Submit a single PDF, US Letter or A4, with embedded fonts.
- The first page must show the paper title, the complete author list, and the
  affiliation footnotes exactly as they should appear in the proceedings.
- Do not include page numbers; the proceedings style adds them.

## Anonymity

Camera-ready manuscripts are **not** anonymous. Author names and affiliations
must be present on the title page.

## Fees, page limits and overlength charges

Registration fees, the manuscript page limit and overlength charges are governed
by the official Presenter Support Policy. Refer to details on
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/knobbyintens/playwright_with_chunk_excel_terminal_arxiv_local_pdf-tools_fetch_filesystem_5569-wfdp26-9d4e7a/main/docs/organizers/edition-2026-support-terms.md

## Upload

Upload the camera-ready PDF through the proceedings repository; the file for
each accepted paper is tracked under `papers/<submission-id>/camera-ready.pdf`.
